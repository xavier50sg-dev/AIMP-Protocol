# AIMP-Protocol
**A Decentralized Research Collective for AI Provenance - Singapore Research Project**

> ⚠️ Research & Educational Purpose Only | Complies with Singapore PDPA & Applicable Laws | Not financial advice | No tokens

## About
AIMP is a Singapore-based open research project exploring how AI-generated content provenance can be tracked and verified in a decentralized manner. This repository is for academic research and technical discussion only.

We comply with Singapore PDPA and all applicable regulations. This is NOT a token, NOT an investment, NOT financial advice.

## Research Goals
- Study decentralized identifiers for AI models
- Explore open standards for content attribution
- Foster community discussion on AI ethics and transparency

## Status
This project is in early research phase. No tokens, no mainnet, no financial products. All content is for research.

## Contributing
Open to researchers, developers, and students interested in AI provenance. Discussions via Issues.
