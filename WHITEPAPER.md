Abstract
AIMP-Protocol (AI Model Provenance Protocol) is a Singapore-based open research initiative exploring decentralized methods for tracking AI-generated content provenance. The goal is to study open standards for transparency and attribution in generative AI.

1. Introduction - Research Problem
With the rise of generative AI, verifying the origin and history of content becomes critical for research integrity, journalism, and education. This project studies how decentralized identifiers (DIDs) and verifiable credentials could help.

2. Research Scope (Not a Product)
This repository is NOT a product, token, or blockchain mainnet. It is:

A literature review collective
An open discussion forum on AI ethics
A prototype for academic purposes (testnet / local only, if any)
No financial incentives, no tokenomics, no fundraising.

3. Proposed Research Areas
3.1 Decentralized Identifiers for AI Models
Study W3C DID standards to assign research identifiers to models for academic tracking.

3.2 Content Attribution Metadata
Explore C2PA and similar open standards for attaching provenance metadata to AI outputs.

3.3 Privacy & Compliance
All research complies with Singapore PDPA. No personal data collection. All examples use synthetic data.
